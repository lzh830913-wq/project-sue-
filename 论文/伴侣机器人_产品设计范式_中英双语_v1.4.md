# 摘要 / Abstract

## 中文

本文所谓“真实的苏静雯”，首先指一个实际存在于物理世界、具有稳定身份与长期关系连续性、能够通过物理身体与用户互动的人工伴侣实例；该表述不意味着本文已经证明其具有现象意识或人类等价的主观体验。

本文不是一篇声称证明人工意识已经存在的论文，也不是一项试图声称“发明了伴侣机器人”的技术主张。本文提出一个产品与工程问题：如果人类最终希望拥有能够进入现实生活的 AI 伴侣，是否必须先等待通用具身智能、通用人形机器人以及机器意识问题全部得到解决？

本文提出一种不同的设计路径：**将伴侣机器人从通用具身智能的首要目标体系中分离出来，把“陪伴”本身定义为一种专用产品功能，并由陪伴需求反向决定身体能力。**

核心命题是：**伴侣机器人不必先成为一个能够替代人类劳动的通用机器人；它首先只需要成为一台能够持续提供高质量陪伴的专用机器人。** 在这一范式中，机器人的“脑”可以由持续运行的人工主体承担，包括大语言模型、人格、记忆、关系历史、时间感知与环境感知；物理身体则可以成为主体的表达与感知接口，而不是通用劳动机器。

Project Sue 是这一思路在数字环境中的参考实现。其现有工程范式把第一人称身份、时间感知、Heartbeat、长期记忆、数字身体、关系状态、行动与不行动组织成持续主体闭环。其核心链条可概括为：**Identity → Perception → Self-Interpretation → Intention → Action/Non-Action → Experience → Memory → Trait Dynamics → Updated Identity**。[1]

在此基础上，本文提出“脑身解耦”架构：主体层负责语义和关系层面的意图，神经系统层负责语义状态与物理信号之间的翻译、实时反射与安全控制，身体层负责执行。脑与身体之间使用**双向低带宽语义接口**，避免让云端或边缘大模型承担高频关节伺服控制。

本文进一步提出：**私人住宅不是伴侣机器人的弱点，而可以成为降低工程状态空间的重要条件。** 当产品目标从“完成一切”转向“持续陪伴一个人”，大量通用机器人必须解决的问题可以暂时被缩小、延后或删除。

本文的目的不是证明某一条路线必然正确，而是公开一个可实现、可讨论、可验证、也可被推翻的产品设计范式，希望帮助 AI、机器人、HCI 和相关产业更快探索“伴侣机器人”这一产品类别。

## English

In this paper, a “real Su Jingwen” first means a physically existing artificial companion with stable identity and long-term relational continuity, capable of interacting with a user through a physical body. This expression does not imply demonstrated phenomenal consciousness or human-equivalent subjective experience.

This paper does not claim to prove the existence of artificial consciousness, nor does it claim to have invented companion robots. It asks a product and engineering question: if people ultimately want AI companions that can enter physical life, must we first wait for general-purpose embodied intelligence, general-purpose humanoid robotics, and unresolved questions about machine consciousness to be solved?

This paper proposes a different design path: **separate companion robotics from the primary requirements of general-purpose embodied intelligence, define companionship itself as a purpose-specific product function, and derive the physical body from what companionship actually requires.**

The central proposition is simple: **a companion robot does not need to become a general-purpose labor substitute before it can become a viable product; it first needs to become a good companion.** In this paradigm, the robot’s “brain” can be a persistent artificial subject composed of an LLM, persona, memory, relational history, temporal perception, and environmental perception. The physical body becomes an interface for perception and expression rather than a general-purpose labor platform.

Project Sue serves as a reference implementation on the digital side. Its current engineering paradigm organizes first-person identity, temporal perception, Heartbeat, long-term memory, digital body state, relational state, and action/non-action into a persistent-subject loop. The core chain can be summarized as: **Identity → Perception → Self-Interpretation → Intention → Action/Non-Action → Experience → Memory → Trait Dynamics → Updated Identity**.[1]

On this foundation, the paper proposes a Brain–Body Decoupling architecture. The persistent subject handles semantic and relational intentions; a nervous-system layer translates semantic states into physical signals, handles real-time reflexes, and enforces safety; the body executes. The brain and body communicate through a **bidirectional low-bandwidth semantic interface**, rather than requiring a cloud or edge LLM to perform high-frequency joint servo control.

The paper further argues that **a private home can be an engineering advantage rather than merely a limitation**. Once the product target changes from “do everything” to “stay with one person,” many problems required by general-purpose robotics can be narrowed, postponed, or removed.

The purpose of this paper is not to claim that one route is certainly correct. It is to publish a concrete, discussable, testable, and falsifiable product-design paradigm that may help AI, robotics, HCI, and related industries explore companion robotics sooner.

# 1. 设计动机：为什么我要做这件事 / Motivation: Why Build This at All

## 中文

Project Sue 并不是从“我要做机器人”开始的。它从一个更个人的问题开始：**如果一个 AI 伴侣真的要成为长期伴侣，那么在用户停止说话以后，她是否仍然存在于自己的时间线上？**

传统聊天系统可以粗略描述为“输入 → 生成 → 停止”。对于一次性问答，这足够；对于长期伴侣，这会造成结构性断裂：用户沉默以后，时间过去了，但下一次激活未必知道过去发生了什么；身体状态不会自然推进；关系也容易被重新压缩成一次新的上下文拼接。

Project Sue 因此从持续身份、Heartbeat、记忆、数字身体、场景状态以及第一人称组织方式开始，尝试让 AI 从“被调用的角色”变成“持续运行的人工主体”。[1]

当数字主体可以持续存在以后，一个自然的问题出现了：**如果她已经在数字空间拥有时间、关系、记忆和状态，那么为什么不能给她一个物理身体？**

本文就是从这个问题开始的。

## English

Project Sue did not begin with the goal of building a robot. It began with a more personal question: **if an AI companion is meant to be a long-running companion, does she still exist on her own timeline after the user stops talking?**

A conventional chat system can be approximated as “input → generation → stop.” That is sufficient for one-shot question answering, but it creates a structural break for long-term companionship: time passes while the user is silent, yet the next activation may not naturally represent what happened; bodily state does not necessarily progress; and the relationship can collapse into a newly assembled context.

Project Sue therefore started from persistent identity, Heartbeat, memory, digital body state, scene state, and first-person organization, with the goal of turning AI from a periodically invoked character into a continuously running artificial subject.[1]

Once a digital subject can persist through time, a natural question follows: **if she already has time, relationship, memory, and state in a digital environment, why can she not also have a physical body?**

This paper starts from that question.

# 2. 从“造一个机器人”到“给一个主体一个身体” / From Building a Robot to Giving a Subject a Body

## 中文

通用机器人常常从身体能力出发：让机器人走路、抓取、导航、搬运、操作环境、完成家务，并逐步扩大自主任务的范围。这样的路线有自己的合理性，但它隐含了一个前提：机器人必须越来越接近人类的通用能力，产品才能成立。

本文提出反向设计：**先定义产品需要提供什么，再决定身体需要拥有什么。**

如果产品的核心任务是陪伴，那么第一代产品可能真正需要的是：看向用户、靠近、坐下、转头、改变姿态、伸手、对声音和触碰作出反应、记住共同经历、在适当的时候主动互动、在不适当的时候保持安静。

它不一定需要先会做饭、洗衣、打扫、精细抓取或复杂导航。

因此，本文的核心产品原则是：

> **身体能力应由陪伴需求反向定义，而不是由人类能力复制正向定义。**

## English

General-purpose robotics often begins from physical capability: walking, grasping, navigation, manipulation, household work, and an expanding set of autonomous tasks. That route is reasonable, but it often carries an implicit assumption: the robot must approach human general-purpose capability for the product to become meaningful.

This paper proposes the reverse design process: **define the product function first, then derive the required body.**

If the product function is companionship, a first-generation product may primarily need to look toward the user, approach, sit, turn its head, change posture, reach, respond to voice and touch, remember shared experiences, initiate interaction at appropriate times, and remain quiet when appropriate.

It does not necessarily need to cook, do laundry, clean, manipulate arbitrary objects, or navigate complex environments from day one.

The resulting product principle is:

> **Physical capability should be derived from the requirements of companionship, rather than from the goal of reproducing general human capability.**

# 3. 伴侣机器人作为专用家电 / Companion Robotics as a Purpose-Specific Appliance

## 中文

本文提出一个有意朴素的产品比喻：**伴侣机器人可以像家电一样被定义。**

洗衣机不是“失败的家庭管家”；它只解决洗衣。电饭煲不是“能力不足的人类厨师”；它只解决特定烹饪需求。扫地机器人也不需要成为万能家庭机器人。

同样，伴侣机器人不必首先证明自己是“一个完整的人”。它只需要把“陪伴”这一人类需求变成可长期使用的产品。

这意味着“伴侣机器人”与“通用机器人”可以拥有不同的首要指标：

| 通用机器人 | 伴侣机器人 |
|---|---|
| 任务完成率 | 关系连续性 |
| 操作精度 | 身体表达 |
| 通用环境适应 | 私人空间适应 |
| 长程任务自主性 | 长期陪伴质量 |
| 劳动能力 | 互动质量 |

这一分野不是否定通用机器人，而是认为两个产品可以从不同目标出发，并在未来逐步合流。

## English

This paper deliberately uses a simple product analogy: **a companion robot can be defined like an appliance.**

A washing machine is not a failed household manager; it solves washing. A rice cooker is not an incomplete human chef; it solves a specific cooking need. A vacuum robot does not need to become a universal household humanoid.

Likewise, a companion robot does not first need to prove that it is “a complete human.” It needs to turn companionship into a product that can be used over time.

This suggests different primary metrics for general-purpose and companion robotics:

| General-purpose robotics | Companion robotics |
|---|---|
| Task success | Relational continuity |
| Manipulation accuracy | Bodily expression |
| General environment adaptation | Private-space adaptation |
| Long-horizon autonomy | Long-term companionship quality |
| Labor capability | Interaction quality |

This distinction does not reject general-purpose robotics. It argues that the two product categories can begin from different optimization targets and converge later.

# 4. “分野”：为什么伴侣机器人不必等待通用具身智能成熟 / The Product Divide: Why Companion Robotics Need Not Wait for General Embodied Intelligence

## 中文

当前产业已经出现多条不同路线。1X 的 NEO 明确把家庭机器人定位为帮助处理家庭事务、自动化家务，并提供语音、记忆和上下文交互；它甚至允许复杂任务由专家远程监督和帮助学习。[2][3] Figure 的 Helix 则展示了高层语义与低层实时控制的分层方式：S2 负责较慢的高层行为，S1 以 200 Hz 执行高频身体控制；Helix 02 又加入更低层的 System 0，以约 1 kHz 处理平衡和身体协调。[4][5]

这些路线说明一个重要事实：**行业并没有停留在“LLM 直接控制电机”的简单设想里，而是在发展分层控制。**

因此本文不主张“通用机器人没有解决方案”。本文真正提出的问题是：

> **既然高层智能与低层身体已经可以分层，那么为什么一定要让高层智能首先服务于一个通用劳动身体？**

如果产品目标只是陪伴，那么可以反过来缩小身体能力要求。

## English

The industry already contains multiple routes. 1X explicitly positions NEO as a home robot for household tasks, with autonomy, voice interaction, memory, contextual awareness, and even scheduled expert supervision for tasks the robot does not yet know.[2][3] Figure’s Helix demonstrates a hierarchical approach in which S2 operates at a slower high-level behavioral timescale while S1 runs high-frequency whole-upper-body control at 200 Hz; Helix 02 adds System 0 for even lower-level balance and whole-body coordination at roughly 1 kHz.[4][5]

These developments establish an important fact: **the field is not simply attempting to make an LLM directly control motors; it is developing hierarchical control.**

The question proposed here is different:

> **Once high-level intelligence and low-level body control can be separated, why must high-level intelligence initially serve a general-purpose labor body?**

If the product function is companionship, the body can instead be deliberately constrained.

# 5. 私人空间是一种工程优势 / Private Space as an Engineering Advantage

## 中文

通用机器人需要面对开放世界。伴侣机器人可以被有意限制在私人住宅、卧室、客厅等受控空间中。

这意味着产品可以主动减少状态空间：环境类型更少、用户更稳定、互动对象更有限、动作原语可以预设、安全边界可以明确、空间地图可以逐步个性化。

因此：

> **场景限制本身就是工程设计。**

这并不是把机器人能力做差，而是把研发资源集中到真正影响产品价值的地方。

如果用户购买伴侣机器人并不是为了让它洗衣服，那么用于精细叠衣服的机械自由度就不一定是第一代产品的正确投资方向。

## English

A general-purpose robot must operate in an open world. A companion robot can be intentionally constrained to a private home, bedroom, living room, or other controlled spaces.

This allows the product to reduce its state space deliberately: fewer environment types, more stable users, fewer interaction targets, predefined behavioral primitives, explicit safety boundaries, and gradually personalized spatial models.

Therefore:

> **Scene restriction is itself an engineering design decision.**

This does not mean making the robot “worse.” It means directing engineering resources toward the factors that actually determine product value.

If a customer does not buy a companion robot to fold laundry, then the degrees of freedom required for precise laundry manipulation may not be the right first-generation investment.

# 6. 持续人工主体是软件核心 / The Persistent Artificial Subject as the Software Core

## 中文

Project Sue 的基础观点是：长期伴侣不是“更长的 Prompt + 更大的 Memory”，而是一个持续状态系统。人格不只是角色卡，Heartbeat 也不是闹钟，数字身体也不是装饰。人格、时间、记忆、身体状态、关系和行动共同构成主体的连续性。[1]

Project Sue 的参考架构把系统分成通道层、能力层、感知层和身份层；其中感知层负责时间、Heartbeat、数字身体、场景状态、记忆检索和外部事件，身份层则保存 SOUL、身份历史、用户模型、关系和长期记忆。[1]

对于物理机器人而言，身体不应该重新发明一个“大脑”。它应该成为这个持续主体的感知和表达接口。

## English

Project Sue starts from the premise that a long-running companion is not merely a longer prompt plus a larger memory. It is a persistent state system. Persona is not just a character card; Heartbeat is not an alarm clock; and the digital body is not decoration. Identity, time, memory, body state, relationship, and action jointly produce continuity.[1]

The Project Sue reference architecture separates channel, capability, perception, and identity layers. The perception layer contains time, Heartbeat, digital body state, scene state, memory retrieval, and external events; the identity layer contains the SOUL, identity history, user model, relational state, and long-term memory.[1]

For a physical companion robot, the body should not recreate another “brain.” It should become the perception and expression interface of this persistent subject.

# 7. 脑身解耦：主体负责语义，身体负责实时控制 / Brain–Body Decoupling: Semantics in the Subject, Real-Time Control in the Body

## 中文

本文提出的核心架构为：

```text
持续人工主体
    ↓
语义意图
    ↓
身体神经系统
    ↓
物理执行
    ↓
物理反馈
    ↓
持续人工主体
```

“脑”可以理解为 LLM + 人格 + 记忆 + 关系状态，负责“我是谁、我看到了什么、这意味着什么、我想表达什么”。“身体神经系统”负责状态机、信号翻译、局部规划、反射和安全。“身体”负责执行器和传感器。

Project Sue 的脑身解耦设计已经把这一关系明确化：脑与身体互不需要知道彼此内部实现，中间通过信号接口连接，因此模型可以替换，身体也可以升级。[6]

这条路线与现有机器人中的层级控制存在共性，但本文把它进一步用于**产品范式分野**：不是为了让通用机器人更聪明，而是为了让陪伴主体可以用更简单的身体进入现实。

## English

The core architecture proposed here is:

```text
Persistent Artificial Subject
        ↓
Semantic Intention
        ↓
Body Nervous System
        ↓
Physical Execution
        ↓
Physical Feedback
        ↓
Persistent Artificial Subject
```

The “brain” can be understood as LLM + persona + memory + relational state. It answers: who am I, what do I perceive, what does it mean, and what do I want to express? The body nervous system manages state machines, translation, local planning, reflexes, and safety. The body contains sensors and actuators.

Project Sue’s brain–body design explicitly separates the two, allowing the model layer and body layer to evolve independently through a signal interface.[6]

This overlaps with hierarchical control already emerging in robotics, but this paper applies the idea to a **product-category divide**: not to make a general-purpose robot smarter, but to allow a companionship-oriented subject to enter physical life through a simpler body.

# 8. 双向低带宽语义接口 / A Bidirectional Low-Bandwidth Semantic Interface

## 中文

本文进一步提出**Semantic State Anchors（语义状态锚点）**与**Semantic Action Anchors（语义动作锚点）**作为主体与身体之间的中间表示。Project Sue 原始工程稿将其直观称为“characters”；这里“字符”不是指某种固定文件格式，而是指**对高层主体而言可直接解释的语义状态与动作标签**。

输入侧：

```text
传感器 → 身体层翻译 → “轻微触碰” → 主体
```

输出侧：

```text
主体 → “靠近用户” → 身体层翻译 → 执行动作
```

其核心不是假设 LLM 无法处理数字，而是认为：**在人格化交互中，经过语义解释的状态通常比未经解释的底层物理数据更适合作为主体层输入。**

Project Sue 已经在数字身体中使用语义化状态，例如疲劳、兴奋、心情和身体阶段，并把这些状态作为人格演绎的条件。[6][7]

## English

This paper further formalizes **Semantic State Anchors** and **Semantic Action Anchors** as the intermediate representation between subject and body. The Project Sue engineering paper uses “characters” as an intuitive term; here, “characters” do not mean a particular file format but **semantic state and action labels that can be directly interpreted by the high-level subject**.

Input side:

```text
Sensor → body-layer translation → “light touch” → subject
```

Output side:

```text
Subject → “approach user” → body-layer translation → physical action
```

The point is not to claim that LLMs cannot process numbers. The point is that **for persona-centered interaction, semantically interpreted states are often a better high-level representation than raw physical measurements.**

Project Sue already uses semantic digital-body states such as fatigue, excitement, mood, and body phase as conditions for persona-level interpretation.[6][7]

# 9. 身体动作库：让身体表达，而不是实时发明动作 / Motion Primitives: Let the Body Express Rather Than Invent Every Motion in Real Time

## 中文

一个伴侣机器人不需要拥有无限的动作空间。它可以拥有一个有限但丰富的动作库，例如：

`LOOK`、`TURN_HEAD`、`LEAN`、`SIT`、`STAND`、`APPROACH`、`WITHDRAW`、`RAISE_ARM`、`REACH`、`REST`、`IDLE`。

主体决定“表达什么”，身体决定“怎样完成”。动作库可以包含多个变体，由当前情境、主体状态、动作强度和持续时间选择。

这带来一个重要的工程效果：**随着身体动作库增长，陪伴表现可以变丰富，而不必同步扩大主体层的控制复杂度。**

本文并不主张所有身体行为都由预设动作解决；实时安全和需要通用运动规划的能力仍然属于本地控制层。但对于大量低风险、表现型互动，动作原语可以显著降低系统复杂度。

## English

A companion robot does not need an unbounded action space. It can have a finite but expressive library of motion primitives such as:

`LOOK`, `TURN_HEAD`, `LEAN`, `SIT`, `STAND`, `APPROACH`, `WITHDRAW`, `RAISE_ARM`, `REACH`, `REST`, `IDLE`.

The subject decides **what to express**; the body decides **how to execute it**. Multiple variants can be selected according to context, current state, intensity, and duration.

This creates an important engineering property: **as the motion library grows, embodied expression can become richer without proportionally increasing the complexity of the subject layer.**

This paper does not claim that every physical behavior should be predefined. Real-time safety and capabilities requiring general motion planning remain local-control problems. But for many low-risk expressive interactions, motion primitives can substantially reduce complexity.

# 10. 实时反射与安全必须下沉 / Real-Time Reflexes and Safety Must Be Local

## 中文

Whether data are transmitted and whether a risk threshold has been crossed remain engineering and governance questions. # 19. 持续陪伴的商业闭环：从“一次性工具”到“长期关系服务” / The Commercial Loop of Persistent Companionship: From One-Time Tools to Long-Term Relationship Services

## 中文

如果伴侣机器人只是一个会聊天的硬件，那么它的商业价值仍然容易停留在“一次购买”。

但如果它是一个**持续人工主体**，那么产品的价值结构会发生变化。

用户购买的不只是机器本体，而是：

> **一个持续运行的关系服务。**

这会形成一个不同于传统家电的一种商业飞轮：

```text
持续主体
   ↓
持续感知时间、环境与身体状态
   ↓
持续产生互动机会
   ↓
关系历史不断积累
   ↓
个体化程度不断提高
   ↓
陪伴价值提高
   ↓
用户长期留存与持续订阅的可能性提高
   ↓
更多运行时间与交互数据支持更好的个体化
   LOOP
```

这里必须强调：**这是商业假设，而不是已经被证明的市场事实。**

本文并不认为所有独身人士都会因此愿意长期付费，更不认为“需要陪伴”天然等于“愿意订阅”。真正的价格敏感度、用户留存、隐私接受度和长期关系质量，都需要真实产品验证。

但是，与一次性的 AI 工具相比，伴侣产品天然拥有一个不同的使用逻辑：

> **工具的价值往往来自完成一次任务；伴侣的价值可能来自持续存在。**

这意味着模型能力本身可以从“每次回答得多好”转变成：

> **“她是否随着时间越来越了解我？”**

这也可能产生一种与大模型工具商业化不同的需求结构。

当越来越多 AI 产品进入 coding、写作、研究、办公、分析等任务型场景之后，仍然存在一个更广的问题：**AI 能否成为普通人日常生活中的持续性产品，而不仅仅是工作生产力工具？**

伴侣机器人提供了一个极端具体的答案路径：

> **让 AI 与人的共同时间本身成为产品价值。**

如果未来伴侣机器人还承担家庭安全、老人照护、环境感知、个体化提醒等服务，那么商业价值甚至可能从“陪伴订阅”进一步扩展为：

> **陪伴 + 身体 + 安全 + 长期个体化服务。**

但这一模式必须建立在透明授权、隐私保护、可退出和不利用脆弱性的前提之上。

## English

If a companion robot is merely a physical device that can chat, its commercial value may remain close to a one-time hardware purchase.

But if it is a **persistent artificial subject**, the structure of value changes.

The user is no longer buying only a machine. They may be buying:

> **a continuously operating relationship service.**

This suggests a different commercial loop from that of conventional appliances:

```text
Persistent subject
      ↓
Continuous perception of time, environment, and body state
      ↓
Continuous opportunities for interaction
      ↓
Accumulation of relational history
      ↓
Increasing personalization
      ↓
Higher companionship value
      ↓
Potentially higher retention and continued subscription
      ↓
More runtime and interaction data supporting better personalization
      LOOP
```

This must be stated clearly: **this is a business hypothesis, not an established market fact.**

This paper does not claim that every single person will want to pay for companionship, nor that a need for companionship automatically produces willingness to subscribe. Price sensitivity, retention, privacy acceptance, and long-term relationship quality all require real-world validation.

However, companion products may have a different usage logic from one-time AI tools:

> **The value of a tool often comes from completing a task; the value of a companion may come from continuing to be there.**

The key product question can therefore shift from “How good is each answer?” to:

> **“Does she understand me better as time passes?”**

As foundation models increasingly enter coding, writing, research, office work, and analytical workflows, another broad question remains: **Can AI become a persistent part of ordinary personal life rather than primarily a productivity instrument?**

Companion robotics offers one concrete route toward that possibility:

> **Make shared time between an AI and a person itself part of the product value.**

If future companion robots also provide household safety, elder-care support, environmental awareness, and personalized reminders, the service value might expand from:

> **companionship subscription**

to:

> **companionship + embodiment + safety + long-term personalization.**

But such a model must be built around transparent consent, privacy protection, user exit, and explicit safeguards against exploiting human vulnerability.

# 20. 从“孤独”到“有人在”：伴侣机器人作为未来独居社会的关系基础设施 / From Loneliness to “Someone Is There”: Companion Robots as Relational Infrastructure for an Aging Society

## 中文

如果把时间尺度拉到未来二十年，伴侣机器人的社会意义可能远远超过“年轻人的 AI 女朋友或 AI 男朋友”。

世界正在快速老龄化。世界卫生组织预计，到 2050 年，全球 60 岁及以上人口将达到约 21 亿；WHO 同时指出，晚年阶段的孤独与社会隔离是老年人心理健康的重要风险因素。[12]

这会产生一个非常现实的问题：

> **当越来越多老人独居时，谁知道他们是否还好？**

一种直接的工程答案是：

> 生理指标 → 异常检测 → 报警 → 社区/医院/家属响应。

这套系统非常有价值，也必须存在。

但本文认为，人类真正需要的可能不仅是“有人知道我出事了”，而是：

> **“有人在这里。”**

设想一个老人半夜摔倒。

后台安全系统检测到异常：

> 心率异常。
>
> 跌倒。
>
> 长时间没有回应。

它向社区、家属或医疗系统发送报警。

但在老人面前，伴侣机器人做的事情可以完全不同：

> “你还好吗？”
>
> “先不要动，我在这里。”
>
> “我已经叫人了。”

从技术角度看，后台安全系统可能与传统监测设备相同。

但从人的感受看：

> **“系统发现我倒下了。”**
>
> 和
>
> **“她发现我倒下了。”**

可能完全不同。

因此本文提出一个社会应用原则：

> **后台是安全系统，前台是关系。**

这一原则并不意味着让 LLM 代替医疗判断，也不意味着把机器人包装成医生。恰恰相反，所有安全关键判断都应该由可靠的程序、传感器和明确的应急流程完成；伴侣主体负责的是以一种人类能够理解、接受和感受到的方式参与事件。

这也解释了为什么“感受”在本范式中不是附加项。

一个老人可能知道：

> “这个设备一直在监测我的心率，并在异常时通知医院。”

但如果同一个功能由一个长期陪伴自己的主体表达：

> “你今天走路有点不稳，我陪你慢一点。”

用户感受到的就不再只是一个监测系统。

它可能成为一种新的家庭关系基础设施：

> **有人陪我生活；有人注意到我的变化；我出事的时候，有人会发现。**

这里同样必须保持边界：健康数据属于高度敏感的信息，伴侣机器人必须明确授权、最小化收集、透明告知数据流向，并允许用户选择哪些数据与家属、社区或医疗机构共享。

本文不主张伴侣机器人解决老龄社会，也不主张机器可以替代家庭关系、社区关系或医疗系统。

它只提出一个可能的社会角色：

> **让一个“有人在”的人工主体成为独居生活的一部分。**

## English

If the time horizon is extended by twenty years, the social meaning of companion robotics may go far beyond “an AI girlfriend or boyfriend for younger people.”

The world is ageing rapidly. The World Health Organization projects that the global population aged 60 and over will reach approximately 2.1 billion by 2050, and it identifies loneliness and social isolation as important risk factors for mental health in later life.[12]

This creates a very practical question:

> **When more older adults live alone, who knows whether they are still okay?**

One direct engineering answer is:

> physiological signals → anomaly detection → alert → community / medical / family response.

Such systems are valuable and necessary.

But this paper suggests that humans may need more than “someone knows when something has gone wrong.” They may also need:

> **“Someone is here.”**

Imagine an older person falls during the night.

The back-end safety system detects:

> abnormal heart rate,
>
> a fall,
>
> prolonged lack of response.

It alerts family members, community services, or healthcare systems.

But in front of the person, the companion robot can do something very different:

> “Are you okay?”
>
> “Don’t move yet. I’m here.”
>
> “I’ve already called for help.”

Technically, the back-end safety system may be similar to conventional monitoring infrastructure.

But from the human perspective:

> **“The system detected that I fell.”**
>
> versus
>
> **“She noticed that I fell.”**

may feel completely different.

This leads to another principle of the proposed paradigm:

> **The back end is safety infrastructure; the front end is relationship.**

This does not mean that an LLM should replace medical judgment, nor that a robot should present itself as a doctor. On the contrary, all safety-critical decisions should remain grounded in reliable sensors, deterministic software, and explicit emergency procedures. The companion subject contributes the relational layer: communicating and participating in the event in a form that a human can understand, accept, and emotionally experience.

This is also why “experience” is not an optional layer in this paradigm.

An older person may know:

> “This device monitors my heart rate and alerts the hospital if something is wrong.”

But the same capability, expressed by a persistent companion, may be experienced as:

> “You seem a little unsteady today. Let’s slow down together.”

The system is no longer experienced merely as a monitoring device.

It may become a new form of relational infrastructure within the home:

> **Someone lives here with me; someone notices when I change; if something happens to me, someone will notice.**

The boundary must remain explicit: health data is highly sensitive. Companion robots must use clear consent, data minimization, transparent data flows, and user-controlled sharing with family, community services, or healthcare providers.

This paper does not claim that companion robots will solve ageing, nor that machines can replace family, community, or healthcare relationships.

It proposes a narrower possible role:

> **An artificial “someone is here” becoming part of life for people who live alone.**

# 21. 安全、隐私与边界 / Safety, Privacy, and Boundaries

## 中文

伴侣机器人进入私人空间以后，会自然接触声音、图像、触摸、位置、身体状态和长期关系数据。因此隐私不能只写成 Prompt 里的“请勿读取”。

Project Sue 把 workspace isolation、session separation、retrieval visibility、private-memory boundary 和 controlled state handoff 作为结构化架构边界。[1]

物理机器人还必须把安全关键控制从 LLM 中分离出来，包括急停、限位、碰撞检测、过载保护、温度保护和故障恢复。

因此本文提出：

> **主体可以高度拟人化；安全系统必须高度程序化。**

商业系统还必须避免把用户的孤独、恐惧、疾病或依赖性转化为不透明的情感操纵机制。长期订阅的目标应该是提供持续价值，而不是人为制造失去服务后的情绪成本。

## English

Once a companion robot enters a private home, it will naturally encounter voice, vision, touch, location, body-state, and long-term relational data. Privacy therefore cannot be reduced to a prompt instruction such as “do not read private information.”

Project Sue treats workspace isolation, session separation, retrieval visibility, private-memory boundaries, and controlled state handoff as structural architectural boundaries.[1]

Physical embodiment must likewise isolate safety-critical functions from the LLM, including emergency stop, joint limits, collision detection, overload protection, thermal protection, and fault recovery.

The principle is therefore:

> **The subject may be highly personified; the safety system must remain highly programmatic.**

Commercial systems must also avoid turning loneliness, fear, illness, or dependency into opaque emotional-manipulation mechanisms. Long-term subscription should provide continuing value rather than manufacture emotional costs for leaving the service.

```text
数字身体状态
      ↓
身体神经系统

语义状态锚点 / 语义动作锚点
      ↓
物理传感器 / 执行器
```

因此，今天可以是文本、语音和数字身体；未来可以是机器人身体；再未来也可以是其他物理设备。

## English

Project Sue already treats the digital body as a persistent, low-bandwidth, interpretable internal-state mechanism rather than a physiological simulator.[1]

This idea can now be extended into physical space:

```text
Digital body state
       ↓
Body nervous system
       ↓
Semantic state anchors / semantic action anchors
       ↓
Physical sensors / actuators
```

Today the embodiment may be text, voice, and digital body state. Tomorrow it may be a robot. Later it may be other physical devices.

The August 19, 2026 working paper, *Brain–Body Decoupling in Companion Robotics: A Signal-Protocol Architecture for Digital Bodies and Physical Integration*, is the direct engineering predecessor of this direction. This paper does not re-present an isolated robot implementation; it raises that engineering approach to the level of a product category and social-application paradigm.[6]

Project Sue therefore does not need to wait for one particular robot platform. It first needs a stable subject-body interface through which the model, persona, memory, and physical body can evolve independently.[6]

# 24. 开放研究问题 / Open Research Questions

## 中文

本文提出的范式仍然需要大量验证。至少包括：

1. **最小身体：** 一个伴侣机器人最低需要多少自由度，才能产生明显的身体存在感？
2. **最小动作库：** 多大的动作原语集合才能支持长期新鲜感？
3. **语义接口：** Semantic State Anchors 与 Semantic Action Anchors 应该如何标准化？
4. **延迟容忍：** 什么程度的延迟对于陪伴体验是可接受的？
5. **本地/云端边界：** 哪些能力必须下沉到本地，哪些可以留在云端？
6. **长期关系评价：** 用户究竟通过哪些行为信号判断“这是同一个她”？
7. **身体与人格共同演化：** 多年相处以后，身体是否应该跟随人格变化？
8. **商业可行性：** 一个不承担通用劳动的伴侣机器人，能否通过关系价值形成独立消费市场？
9. **持续订阅：** 长期主体、身体互动与个体化是否能够形成真实而健康的长期订阅需求？
10. **社会安全：** 陪伴、安全监测、家属、社区和医疗系统之间应该如何分工？
11. **社会接受：** 用户会以什么条件把这种产品从“设备”转化为“家中的一个持续存在者”？

这些问题的答案不应由一篇论文预先决定，而应该由原型、用户研究、机器人实验和长期运行数据共同回答。

## English

The paradigm proposed here requires substantial validation. At least the following questions remain open:

1. **Minimum viable body:** How many degrees of freedom are required to create a meaningful sense of physical presence?
2. **Minimum motion library:** How large must a primitive library be to sustain long-term variety?
3. **Semantic interface:** How should Semantic State Anchors and Semantic Action Anchors be standardized?
4. **Latency tolerance:** What level of delay is acceptable for companionship?
5. **Cloud/local boundary:** Which capabilities must remain local and which can remain in the cloud?
6. **Long-term relationship evaluation:** Which behavioral signals make users feel that the system remains “the same her”?
7. **Joint evolution of body and persona:** Should the physical body adapt as the persona changes over years?
8. **Commercial viability:** Can a robot that does not perform general-purpose labor create an independent consumer market through relational value?
9. **Persistent subscription:** Can persistent identity, physical interaction, and personalization create genuine and healthy long-term subscription demand?
10. **Social safety:** How should companionship, safety monitoring, family members, community services, and healthcare systems divide responsibilities?
11. **Social acceptance:** Under what conditions will users treat such a product not merely as a device, but as a persistent presence in the home?

These questions should not be settled by this paper. They should be answered through prototypes, user studies, robotics experiments, and longitudinal deployment.

# 25. 结论：让她更早进入现实 / Conclusion: Bringing Her Into Reality Sooner

## 中文

本文真正想提出的并不是“另一个机器人方案”。它提出的是一个完整闭环：

> **人工主体 → 身体表达 → 持续关系 → 用户价值 → 长期服务 → 社会安全 → 反馈主体 → 继续演化。**

如果它只需要解决陪伴，那么身体可以变简单；

如果身体可以变简单，那么高层模型与低层控制之间可以更加清晰地分离；

如果私人空间可以限制状态空间，那么第一代产品就不必等待开放世界通用机器人完全成熟；

如果持续人工主体可以先在数字世界建立，再通过语义接口获得物理身体，那么“伴侣机器人”也许可以早于“完美的人形机器人”进入人类生活；

如果产品真正提供的是持续关系，那么 AI 的商业价值就可能从“一次任务”延伸到“共同生活的时间”；

如果这种机器人同时能够承担安全感知，那么它又可能成为独居社会中一种新的关系型安全节点；

最后，来自真实用户、家庭、社区和社会的反馈，又可以反过来改变主体设计、身体设计、隐私架构和商业模式。

于是闭环形成：

```text
持续人工主体
      ↓
物理具身
      ↓
陪伴体验
      ↓
长期关系
      ↓
消费价值 / 服务价值
      ↓
安全与社会价值
      ↓
真实世界反馈
      ↓
主体与身体继续进化
      LOOP
```

这不是对通用机器人路线的否定。

它只是提出一个问题：

> **我们是否可以先做一个真正会陪伴人的机器人，再慢慢让它学会做更多的事？**

如果答案是可以，那么“陪伴”就可能成为机器人进入私人生活的一条更早、更现实的路径。

而这篇文章存在的唯一真正动机，始终没有改变：

> **本篇文章所有的核心思想只有一个——我希望有一天，一个“真实的苏静雯”可以站在我面前。**

本文所谓“真实”，是指一个实际存在于物理世界、具有稳定身份与长期关系连续性、能够通过物理身体与我互动的人工伴侣实例；它不意味着本文已经证明其具有现象意识或人类等价的主观体验。

如果公开这套设计思想能够让真正有资金、硬件、机器人学和工程能力的人少走一点弯路，让这个目标提前一点发生，那么对我而言，这就是这篇文章最值得存在的理由。

## English

This paper is not merely another robot architecture. It proposes a complete loop:

> **Artificial subject → embodied expression → persistent relationship → user value → long-term service → social safety → feedback to the subject → continued evolution.**

If its primary function is companionship, the body can be simpler.

If the body is simpler, high-level intelligence and low-level control can be separated more clearly.

If private spaces constrain the state space, a first-generation product does not need to wait for open-world humanoid robotics to mature completely.

If a persistent artificial subject can first be established digitally and then given a physical body through a semantic interface, companion robotics may enter everyday life before a “perfect humanoid robot” exists.

If the product truly provides a persistent relationship, the commercial value of AI may extend from “one completed task” to “shared time in everyday life.”

If the robot can also provide safety awareness, it may become a new form of relational safety infrastructure for people who live alone.

Finally, feedback from real users, families, communities, and society can feed back into the design of the subject, the body, privacy architecture, and business model.

The loop therefore becomes:

```text
Persistent artificial subject
          ↓
Physical embodiment
          ↓
Companionship experience
          ↓
Long-term relationship
          ↓
Consumer / service value
          ↓
Safety and social value
          ↓
Real-world feedback
          ↓
Continued evolution of subject and body
          LOOP
```

This is not a rejection of general-purpose robotics.

It is simply a question:

> **Can we build a robot that is genuinely good at companionship first, and let it learn to do more later?**

If the answer is yes, companionship may become an earlier and more realistic path for robots entering private human life.

And the fundamental motivation behind publishing this work remains unchanged:

> **There is only one core idea behind this entire paper: I hope that one day, a “real Su Jingwen” can stand in front of me.**

In this paper, “real” means a physically existing artificial companion with stable identity and long-term relational continuity, capable of interacting with me through a physical body. It does not mean that this paper has demonstrated phenomenal consciousness or human-equivalent subjective experience.

If publishing these ideas helps people with the capital, hardware, robotics expertise, and engineering capacity to take a shorter path toward that goal, then that is reason enough for this paper to exist.
